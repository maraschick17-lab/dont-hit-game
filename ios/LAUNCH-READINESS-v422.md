## Privacy policy draft — not published
Rush to Orbit requires internet to start and resume play. No account is required. The game checks a first-party HTTPS marker to verify reachability. Settings, unlocks, liveries, personal campaign/Endless records and daily records are stored locally. If you choose to post a standard campaign finish, a short nickname (1–3 letters/digits), random per-run ID and four stage scores/times are sent to our score service. The service retains nickname, ID, total points and elapsed milliseconds to rank and deduplicate runs; the public top 25 lists nickname, score and time. Do not enter real names or identifying information. A pending submission may remain on your device for retry. Clearing local data does not remove scores already posted. Hosting infrastructure processes connection information when requests are made. No advertising, in-app purchases, third-party analytics SDK or tracking is implemented in this candidate. No guarantees of anonymity, automatic deletion or fixed retention deadlines.
